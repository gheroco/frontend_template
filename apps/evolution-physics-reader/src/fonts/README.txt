Book Symbols and Book Math

Self-hosted WOFF2 subsets of Noto Serif Regular/Italic and Noto Sans Math Regular, respectively. Source: Debian fonts-noto-core, /usr/share/fonts/truetype/noto/. Copyright 2010, 2012–2020 Google Inc.; 2015–2020 Google LLC. Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; see NOTO-OFL-LICENSE.txt. Modified subsets use independent family names.

The unicode ranges in style.css contain only Greek, mathematical symbols, superscripts, subscripts, arrows and related operators. Ordinary Latin/Cyrillic text uses the existing reader fonts. Noto Serif supplies glyphs missing from Noto Sans Math, including U+210F, superscripts and subscripts. Noto Sans Math supplies supplemental operators including U+2A73 and U+2A7E.

Generated with fontTools.subset from the stated system font sources. The regular/italic Noto Serif pair preserves source italic notation. Font glyph coverage was checked after WOFF2 serialization.
